Our Story

From Garden Frustration
to EdgeMaster

We believe the best tools are the ones that let you forget you're holding them. The EdgeMaster Pro was born from a simple observation: gas-powered edgers are noisy, messy, and over-engineered for what should be a peaceful weekend ritual.

After months of prototyping with professional landscapers and backyard gardeners alike, we created something different — a tool that makes edging feel effortless. The wide foot pedal transfers your weight naturally. The titanium blade glides through turf. The ergonomic handle becomes an extension of your arm.

No batteries to charge. No fuel to mix. No cords to untangle. Just you, the garden, and the satisfying sound of a clean cut.

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before you buy.

How is this different from a gas edger? ▾
Everything. No gas, no oil, no noise, no fumes. The EdgeMaster Pro uses your body weight to do the work — it's lighter, quieter, and requires zero maintenance. Perfect for early mornings and peaceful weekends.
Is it difficult to use? ▾
Not at all. The wide foot pedal and ergonomic handle make it intuitive. Most users get the hang of it in their first pass. It's designed to feel natural — like an extension of your leg.
How long does the blade stay sharp? ▾
The titanium alloy blade stays sharp 3× longer than standard steel blades. We also include 2 spare blades in the package, so you're covered for multiple seasons of regular use.
What kind of lawns does it work on? ▾
The EdgeMaster Pro works on most standard residential lawns with common grass types (Bermuda, Fescue, Zoysia, Kentucky Blue, etc.). For hard or rocky soil, we recommend edging after rain or watering.
Do I need to assemble it? ▾
No. The EdgeMaster Pro comes fully assembled. Just unwrap it and start edging. We believe your time is better spent in the garden, not reading instruction manuals.
Can I return it if I don't like it? ▾
Absolutely. We offer a 30-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. If the EdgeMaster Pro doesn't transform your lawn care routine, send it back for a full refund.
